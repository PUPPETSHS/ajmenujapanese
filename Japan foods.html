How to eat like a local

Slurp your noodles

Slurping ramen, udon and soba is normal. It cools the noodles and brings out the broth’s aroma.

Say itadakimasu

Say it before the first bite, and “gochisousama deshita” when you finish, to thank the cook.

Mind your chopsticks

Never stand them upright in rice, and do not pass food from chopsticks to chopsticks.

Dip, do not drown

Touch the fish side of sushi to soy sauce, not the rice, so the rice stays together.

Try the small counters

Tiny counter-only shops often cook one dish very well. Buy a ticket from the machine and sit down.

Eat with the seasons

Oden in winter, cold soba in summer, mochi in spring. Menus change with the weather.
